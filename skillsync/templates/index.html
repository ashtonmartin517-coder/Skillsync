How it works

Check

Answer 13 quick questions. No judging. It takes about 3 minutes.

Get a 4-week roadmap built from your gaps, with prerequisites first.

Learn

Every term comes with an everyday analogy. Finish tasks, earn XP, keep your streak.

Connect

Find peers who are strong where you are weak, and help them back.
